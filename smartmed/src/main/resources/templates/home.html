ABOUT US

At SmartMed, we understand that visiting a doctor can sometimes feel intimidating, uncomfortable, or even embarrassing. Many people hesitate to discuss their symptoms openly, which can lead to delays in seeking medical advice. That’s why we created this platform—a healthcare assistant designed to provide a more private and convenient way to assess your symptoms and receive preliminary health insights.

This project is developed by a university student who believes that technology can bridge the gap between people and healthcare. While this is just the beginning, we are continuously working to improve and expand the platform. We hope that SmartMed will empower individuals to take control of their health with confidence and ease.
